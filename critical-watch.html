Critical Watch

Sepsis early warning for a 12-bed medical ward. Risk is re-scored every hour from vitals and labs, so deterioration shows up before the bedside team would spot it.

All patients, vitals and model figures here are synthetic and generated in your browser. This is a design prototype, not a medical device. Do not use it for clinical decisions.

    This demo uses a transparent logistic score over heart rate, respiratory rate, temperature, blood pressure, SpO₂, lactate, white cell count and age. Each patient’s drivers are the terms that moved their score most. Replace it with your validated model.
